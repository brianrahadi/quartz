## software 
applications:
  - [things 3](https://culturedcode.com/things/) - to-do list
  - [obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - all-in-one note-taking, integrated with this website with most files gitignored
  - [forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/) - plant trees. leave the app -> trees ded.
  - [one sec](https://one-sec.app/) - wait 12s to open distracting apps
  - [freedom](https://freedom.to/) - block distracting apps most of the time
  - [kindle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amazon-kindle/id302584613) - make the brain cooked well 
  - [snipd](https://www.snipd.com/) - read podcast easily. intuitive highlights
  - [readwise](https://readwise.io/) - aggregate important stuff into digestable stuf

chrome extensions:
  - [freedom](https://freedom.to/) - block distracting apps most of the time
  - [video speed controller](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/video-speed-controller/nffaoalbilbmmfgbnbgppjihopabppdk?hl=en) - control video speeed
  - [distraction free youtube](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/df-tube-distraction-free/mjdepdfccjgcndkmemponafgioodelna?hl=en) - remove youtube feeds and shorts
  - [news feed eradicator](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/news-feed-eradicator/fjcldmjmjhkklehbacihaiopjklihlgg?hl=en) - remove most social media feeds
  - [simplify](https://simplify.jobs/dashboard) - apply jobs fast
