The initial version of Bun was written by me in 1 year, in a cramped Oakland apartment, pre-LLM, in Zig. The default outcome for ambitiously-scoped projects like Bun is joining the graveyard of dead side projects on a GitHub profile page. Zig made Bun possible.

Nowadays, Bun's CLI gets over 22 million monthly downloads.
