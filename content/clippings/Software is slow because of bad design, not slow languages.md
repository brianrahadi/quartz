> Most programs cannot be meaningfully sped up by rewriting them in C or Rust.
>
> Software performance is usually dominated by I/O, not the speed of the language.
>
> In general, performance is a matter of design, not of language choice.
>
> The set of programs that can be meaningfully sped up by this kind of rewrite is very narrow.
>
> But in terms of raw speed, you’re better off focusing on the program’s design.
