This is Pele, my new rendering library for diagrams and charts, compatible with the latest Mermaid syntax.

\- 100-500× faster than mermaid.js

\- 6× smaller bundle

\- No dependencies

\- Easy to theme

\- Responsive to device width

In pursuit of making Obsidian insanely fast and cohesively designed, I always found the Mermaid library to be too big, too slow, and too hard to theme. Pele will be the new renderer in the next version of Obsidian, but you can start using it now.

Pele \[ˈpɛlɛ\] is named after the goddess of volcanoes and fire. It's open source under the MIT license. Try the playground!
